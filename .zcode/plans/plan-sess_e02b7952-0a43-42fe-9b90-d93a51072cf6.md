## Step 1 — Design preview (you pick the winner)
I hand-craft **3 SVG variants** of the concept and render them in an inline preview (large size + 32px favicon size + dark/light, next to the "Resume Roaster" wordmark in Bricolage) using the session visualization panel. All three: geometric, no faces, no text, reads at 16px, recolor-safe.

- **V1 — "Ember Fold" (refined negative space):** a confident asymmetric flame (3 tongues, leaning like it's moving) with the page knocked out as *pure white negative space* — no person icon, no text lines inside (that detail is what makes your generated version look like stock clipart). - **V2 — "Consumed":** the resume page tilted, with flame tongues rising from below *eating* its bottom edge — narrative "your resume is burning" composition, more dynamic.
- **V3 — "The page is the fire":** single shape — the page's top edge itself morphs into flame tongues. No nested cutouts at all; the boldest and most unusual silhouette.
